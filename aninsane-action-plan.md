# Aninsane — Action Plan

**Context:** Full-time game illustrator job (2-4hr/day, flexible). Need additional income within weeks/months. Long-term goal: rig-ready VTuber/OC model art (human-durable vs. illustration, which faces AI pressure). Illustration is the funnel, not the end goal. Budget: 3-4 hrs/day for all side work.

**Priority order:** Inkoleia competition → Aninsane brand launch → Hobby game project (parked).

---

## Phase 1: Finish Inkoleia — 2 weeks, hard cap
Fix only these three, in order, then stop:
1. Hair — restore no-direct-light color shift (your core signature, currently underbaked)
2. Warm/cool split — add one warm source touching her (currently all-cool)
3. Foreground blur on the mushrooms so they recede instead of competing for focus

No further revision passes beyond this list. Finished > perfect — this piece needs to launch the brand, not define your ceiling.

---

## Phase 2: Brand launch sprint — 1-2 weeks after Phase 1
- No new art this phase — curate only.
- Sample set: Inkoleia + dragon guy + chained guy (+1 more if available).
- Write plain-language offering description.
- Post to OC/VTuber-adjacent spaces (Twitter/Bluesky). Skip VGen for now — too complex for zero client base.
- Open **2-3 premium slots**, not a long waitlist.

---

## Phase 3: First clients — ongoing
- Deliver the open slots well.
- Ask permission to post client reactions as testimonials (your real proof points).
- Re-open in small batches as capacity allows. Scale price before you scale volume.

---

## Phase 4: Rig-ready test piece — after 1-2 illustration clients land
- One test only: rebuild an existing piece (dragon guy or chained guy) with real rig-layer separation.
- Answers the single open question the VTuber pivot depends on: does the soft hair color-shift survive part separation?
- This is prep for the long-term flagship — not required to produce income yet, just needs to exist before you lean into VTuber positioning.

---

## Parked
**Hobby simulator game** — weekends only, once Phase 3 is stable. Never competes with weekday income-critical hours.

## Open tension to watch
Premium/painterly positioning is inherently slower to monetize than volume work. If income pressure spikes before Phase 3 lands a client, revisit — don't quietly dilute the brand to chase faster cash without deciding to.
