# Aninsane — Brand Foundation

*Layer 1 (Positioning) & Layer 2 (Differentiators)*

---

## Layer 1: Positioning Statement

> Aninsane creates premium character art for OC owners and VTuber creators whose characters are marked by divine or mythical origin — dragon-blood, angelic or demonic nature, goddess-touched — where that mark carries real narrative weight, not just decoration. Each character is rendered intimately within an environment that reflects their story, using soft, ambient light instead of harsh direct highlights.

**Who it's for:** OC owners and VTuber creators — people who treat their character as an extension of their own identity, not just content.

**Why it matters to them:** they aren't buying "a nice picture." They're buying proof that their character is as meaningful as they already feel it is.

**Reason to believe:** a consistent rendering philosophy and format across real portfolio pieces, not just a claimed aesthetic.

---

## Layer 2: Differentiator Stack

| Differentiator | Status | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Rendering philosophy** | ✅ Proven | Soft, ambient color/value shifts within a narrow hue range, instead of harsh direct specular highlights — even in dramatic lighting. Demonstrated consistently across multiple pieces (bridal, cooking, silver-haired action piece). |
| **Format** | 🔶 Emerging | Intimate, character-in-environment "key-visual" style — not flat, background-less splash art. Environment scales in intensity to match the piece's emotional message. Currently backed by 2 strong pieces (dragon guy, chained guy), not yet a full track record. |
| **Thematic lane** | 🔶 Emerging | Characters marked by divine or mythical origin — dragon-blood, angelic/demonic nature, goddess-touched — where the mark is a genuine blessing-or-curse, not decoration. Resonates personally, backed by 2 pieces; angels/demons and forest goddess still unproven in practice. |
| **Service scope** | ✅ Proven (illustration) | Illustration is the flagship, proven service (real gacha-style fanart + paid OC commissions). Character design sheets and VTuber-ready model art are real but early-stage — positioned as growing specialties, not equal-billing yet. |
| **Tier** | Decision | Premium, painterly — fewer, stronger pieces over high volume. |

---

## Honest caveats (don't skip these)

- **The theme alone is not rare** — divine/mythical marks with moral weight is a known trope in fantasy illustration. The *combination* of theme + rendering philosophy + format is what's actually hard to copy.
- **Format and theme are hypotheses, not yet consistency.** Both are backed by 1–2 pieces you're proud of, not a repeated track record. The honest next step is a deliberate practice series (3–5 pieces) testing whether the combination holds up across different characters, moods, and even other people's OC designs — not more branding discussion.
- **Personality/voice (Layer 3) is only lightly sketched so far:** "quiet, thoughtful, a little reverent" — tied to the Aninsane name's own sanity/insanity duality and the Einstein-quote idea of returning to the same obsessive question until it's rendered right. This still needs to be built out deliberately (captions, process-sharing, how you talk about pieces).

---

## What's next

Layer 1 and 2 have a real, evidence-traceable shape now — stronger than "gacha-style artist for OC owners," because every piece of the stack is checkable against actual work, not just aspiration. The recommended next move is **practice before more branding**: build the 3–5 piece series testing the rendering + format + theme combination, then return to fully build out Layer 3 (personality/voice) once there's more proof behind the identity.
